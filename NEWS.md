# huncMarkovABD News

## huncMarkovABD 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* 5-state tunnel-state Markov model: Lunavar+SoC vs SoC.
* Three flare-up tunnel states (mild, moderate, severe).
* 2-week cycle length, 50-year horizon.
* Age-dependent background mortality (Canadian life table, 55% female).
* Per-severity treatment RR effects on flare-up rates.
* PSA with Beta, Gamma, LogNormal, and Utility distributions.
* Interactive Shiny app with parameter tabs, CE plane, CEAC, and trace.
* Developed using HEPackageR skill for Claude AI.
